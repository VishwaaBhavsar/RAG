# Modern Minimalist Theme

Clean, geometric, professional aesthetic with restrained color palette.

## Characteristics
- **Style**: Clean lines, generous whitespace, geometric shapes
- **Mood**: Professional, calm, focused
- **Best For**: SaaS, B2B landing pages, dashboards, professional portfolios
- **Audience**: Business professionals, tech-savvy users

## Color Psychology
- **Primary Blue**: Trust, reliability, professionalism
- **Neutral Grays**: Sophistication, balance, clarity
- **Minimal Accent**: Strategic emphasis without distraction

## Usage Guidelines

### 60-30-10 Distribution
- **60% Background**: `--color-background`, `--color-card`
- **30% Secondary**: `--color-muted`, `--color-gray-*`
- **10% Primary**: `--color-primary` for CTAs and key actions

### Typography Pairing
- **Headings**: Plus Jakarta Sans (geometric, modern)
- **Body**: Inter (highly readable, neutral)
- **Code**: JetBrains Mono

### Animation Intensity
- **Subtle**: 150-200ms transitions
- **Easing**: `ease-out` for natural feel
- **Hover states**: Slight opacity or color shift

### Best Practices
- Use generous whitespace
- Maintain strict grid alignment
- Limit decorative elements
- Let content breathe
